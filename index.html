Itorero ry'Imbyino Gakondo (Imyaka 2–15)

Tuzamura Impano z'abana
Mu mbyino gakondo

Agasaro Ballet ni itsinda abana bato batorezwamo umuco nyarwanda batarama Kinyarwanda (Kubyina imbyino gakondo, kuririmba, kuvuza ingoma, ibyivugo, n'amateka). Batozwa mu biruhuko bikuru.

Andikisha Umwana Ubu Reba amashusho
Buri rugo rugire Intore

Umuco wacu, Ishema ryacu

Intore A (Imyaka 2–5)

Kugenda buhoro no kubyina mu njyana z'umuziki, bigisha abana gutega amaboko by'ibanze, no gukurikiza injyana neza.

Intore B (Imyaka 6–10)

Kwiga imyanya myiza na Umushayizero ku bakobwa, n'imisimbuko ya Intore ku bahungu.

Intore C (Imyaka 11–15)

Kwigira imbyino gakondo mu buryo bwuzuye, guhuza injyana n’ingoma za kinyarwanda.

Term 3 Special Mark & Program

Ibikorwa by'Igihembwe cya 3 (Term 3 Activities)

Andikisha umwana

Mu biruhuko bikuru by'igihembwe cya 3, abana bacu bakora ibikorwa bitandukanye bizamura impano zabo n'ubumenyi bw'umuco nyarwanda.

Imbyino n'Ingoma

Kwiga neza kubyina Umushayizero, imisimbuko ya Intore, guhuza intambwe n'ingoma n'amajwi y'ubusizi.

Ibyivugo & Amateka

Gutoza abana gutinyuka mu ruhame, kuririmba indirimbo gakondo, n'amateka meza y'umuco w'u Rwanda.

Ibitaramo no Guhemba

Kugaragaza impano mu bitaramo biterwa ku mpera z'ibiruhuko no guhemba abana bazamuye ubumenyi cyane.

Ubusabane

Kurema ubumwe n'ubwiyunge mu bana bato, kubaha abakuru, n'ubupfura buranga Intore nyakuri.
